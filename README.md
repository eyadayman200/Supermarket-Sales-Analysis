# Supermarket Sales Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing supermarket sales data to evaluate sales performance, understand product profitability, and identify business insights using data analysis and visualization techniques.

The goal is to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights that support better business decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profitability.
* Identify top-performing product lines.
* Track monthly sales performance.
* Evaluate gross profit and profit margins.
* Identify sales trends and potential business opportunities.
* Build an interactive dashboard for business stakeholders.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Microsoft Excel:** Data preparation, Pivot Tables, and reporting.
* **SQL Server:** Data querying, aggregation, and KPI calculations.
* **Python:** Data analysis and exploratory data analysis.
* **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards and data visualization.
* **DAX:** Measures and business calculations.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* Total Sales / Net Sales
* Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
* Gross Profit
* Net Profit
* Gross Profit Margin
* Net Profit Margin
* Quantity Sold
* Return Rate

## 📈 Dashboard Features

* Sales performance overview.
* Product line sales and profitability analysis.
* Monthly sales trends.
* Profit and margin analysis.
* Product return analysis.
* Interactive filters for exploring business performance.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

The project explores:

* Best-performing product lines by sales.
* Product lines generating the highest profit.
* Monthly sales fluctuations.
* Product return patterns.
* Areas that may require further business investigation.

*Specific findings and recommendations are documented in the project report.*

## 📂 Project Structure

* `Dataset/` — Source dataset.
* `Excel/` — Excel analysis and dashboard.
* `PowerBI/` — Power BI report file.
* `SQL/` — SQL queries and KPI calculations.
* `Python/` — Python analysis scripts or notebooks.
* `Screenshots/` — Dashboard previews.
* `Report/` — Final project report.
